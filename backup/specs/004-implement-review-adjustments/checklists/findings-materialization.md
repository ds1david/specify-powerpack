# Checklist: Finding Materialization

- [ ] Every accepted finding has a stable id and reviewed snapshot reference.
- [ ] Every accepted finding identifies its selected delivery target and affected requirement/acceptance item.
- [ ] Every accepted finding states required repair and closure evidence.
- [ ] Implementation/test/configuration/documentation/executable-verification findings are represented in active-SPEC `tasks.md`.
- [ ] Requirements-quality/coverage/ambiguity/consistency/compliance findings update an applicable checklist or authoritative artifact when needed.
- [ ] Missing product intent is routed to clarification/decision rather than invented by the reviewer.
- [ ] Finding closure is traceable from review -> artifact work -> implementation/evidence -> fresh review.
